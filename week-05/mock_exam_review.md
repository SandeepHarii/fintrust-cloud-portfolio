# Performance Analysis

The mock exam showed that my biggest challenge was not only understanding AWS concepts but also answering long scenario-based questions efficiently.

I spent too much time analysing early questions and carefully comparing long answer options. By the final section I had less than one minute remaining, forcing me to guess several questions.

After reviewing the exam afterwards, I recognised that many of those guessed questions were actually within my ability and could likely have been answered correctly if I had managed my time better.

# Lessons Learned

This mock exam reinforced several important lessons:

- Time management is just as important as technical knowledge.
- Long scenario questions require identifying AWS keywords quickly.
- Understanding why AWS recommends a service is more valuable than memorising service names.
- Architecture diagrams help visualise solutions and speed up decision-making during the exam.
